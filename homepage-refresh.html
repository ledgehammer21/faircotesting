Landscape Development

Developers and homebuilders bring Fairco in to build the landscape: construction and project management for master-planned communities, multifamily, resorts, military housing and parks. Builders across three states have trusted that work to Fairco since 1978.

Services include pre-construction, landscape construction, project management and native salvage.

Landscape Management

Recurring care for HOAs, property managers and the commercial properties they answer for. The same stewards work your property every visit, and we manage every account against a water budget you can see month to month.

Services include landscape maintenance, water management, plant health care and enhancements.

Arbor Resources

Your trees are an asset. Our ISA Certified Arborists care for them across established communities and commercial properties, inventorying each tree with GIS mapping and pruning every species on its own clock.

Services include tree inventory, risk assessment, structural pruning and storm-damage cleanup.

About Us

Fairco is a family-owned landscape company. Gene Bearinger founded it in 1978 and still owns it today, and our crews have built landscape infrastructure for master-planned communities, multifamily complexes, resorts, casinos, military housing and parks across Arizona, California and Nevada.

That work taught us what a landscape needs once the build crews leave. Our Landscape Management and Arbor Resources teams bring that knowledge to communities and properties already in place, led by people who have run maintenance and tree programs at scale.
