Fairco Landscape Group

Fairco Landscape Group is the parent company of Fairco Landscape Development and Fairco Landscape Management. We're family-owned and licensed in Arizona, California and Nevada, with our headquarters in Phoenix, an operations center in Litchfield Park and an office in San Diego.

We stand for work that holds up. Our crews self-perform the landscape and irrigation work, our safety record runs at a 0.66 EMR against an industry standard of 1.0, and the people who started projects with us decades ago are still on them today.

Our Story

Gene Bearinger founded Fairco in 1978, and the company has stayed under the same family ownership ever since. For most of those years, our work has been building landscape infrastructure for the developers and homebuilders behind the Southwest's master-planned communities: irrigation mainlines, lakes and pump stations, parks, streetscapes and the planting that ties them together.

Those communities need care for decades after the build crews leave. Fairco Landscape Group is our next chapter: the same family ownership and the same standard, now covering the life of a landscape from the first site walk through long-term management.

Our Vision

Building a landscape and maintaining it usually fall to two different companies. When something goes wrong between installation and upkeep, each one points at the other.

Fairco Landscape Group puts both under one name and one standard. The team managing your property can call the team that built it, and nobody has a reason to pass the blame.

Our Philosophy

We treat every landscape as an investment that has to perform for decades. We develop it to last, manage it the way an owner would, enhance it as the property's needs change, and protect what took years to grow.

Landscape Development

Developers and homebuilders bring Fairco in to build the landscape, from pre-construction and native plant salvage through irrigation, lakes and pump stations, and final installation. Our own crews self-perform the work on master-planned communities, multifamily, resort and military housing projects across three states.

Landscape Management

Recurring care for HOAs, community associations and property managers, run as one program so the turf, the trees and the water are managed together. The same stewards work your property every visit, and we document the work so your board knows exactly what got done.

Services include landscape maintenance, Arbor Resources, plant health care, irrigation and water management and landscape enhancements.
