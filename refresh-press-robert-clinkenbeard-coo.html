Fairco names Robert Clinkenbeard Chief Operating Officer

Fairco Landscape Group has named Robert Clinkenbeard Chief Operating Officer and Strategic Growth Partner. He will lead the company's expansion from landscape development into landscape management, water management, tree care and plant health care across the Southwest.

His focus is uniting Fairco's development and maintenance operations so clients work with one team for the life of a landscape. For the HOAs and property managers who take over the communities Fairco builds, the company that installed the landscape can now care for it.

Clinkenbeard brings 40 years in landscape contract management, including building and selling a $21 million maintenance company. He is a past president of the Arizona Landscape Contractors Association.
