The standard we work to

ANSI and ISA standards on every tree

All work is performed to current ANSI A300 pruning standards, ANSI Z133 safety requirements and ISA Best Management Practices. Tree work is directed by ISA Certified Arborists and executed by ISA Certified Tree Workers.

  • Natural-form pruning only. Topping is never performed.
  • Corrective and thinning cuts are distributed evenly to preserve the tree's natural outline.
  • Tools are kept sharp and sterilized between cuts whenever disease is suspected.
  • Palms are trimmed with tools sterilized between palms to prevent Fusarium transmission.
  • New and replacement trees are staked to allow natural movement for strong trunk development.
  • Integrated Pest Management comes first, with chemical treatment only where warranted.
A program built on data

Each species, in its right season.

Pruning every tree every year inflates cost and can harm tree health. Fairco manages trees on a species-based, cyclical schedule, with each species worked in the window that is horticulturally correct for it.

A GIS-based inventory records species, trunk diameter, height and spread, condition rating, location and a running maintenance log for every tree.

Data-driven condition ratings turn tree care from a reactive expense into a planned, budgetable asset-management program.

Risk assessment

ISA Tree Risk Assessment, at three levels

Hazard evaluation follows the ISA Tree Risk Assessment Qualified (TRAQ) methodology.

Level 1

A limited visual scan on every routine visit.

Level 2

A basic ground assessment during the annual inventory evaluation.

Level 3

An advanced assessment where elevated risk is identified.

Trees and water

Right tree, right place, right water.

Right tree, right place

Replacements favor native and desert-adapted species such as mesquite, palo verde, ironwood and desert willow.

Healthy trees use water well

Properly pruned, structurally sound trees establish faster, resist stress and use applied water more efficiently.

Deep, infrequent watering

We advise on emitter placement and deep-watering cycles that push roots down and improve drought resilience.

Green waste returned as mulch

Chipped material can be returned as mulch to conserve soil moisture and moderate root-zone temperature.
