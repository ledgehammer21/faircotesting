Manage the water. Improve the landscape.

Fairco approaches water as both a landscape resource and a significant operating expense. We combine system inspections, water budgeting, irrigation technology and ongoing field observations to proactively manage consumption while supporting plant, turf and tree performance.

How we work

How we manage water

Water usage budget

A month-by-month budget built from reference evapotranspiration and plant coefficients, converted to gallons and cost. Actual usage is logged against it every month.

ET run-time calculator

Run times calculated from reference ET, precipitation rate, distribution uniformity, soil intake rate and root-zone depth, for each station.

Seasonal landscape calendar

A 52-week calendar that sequences irrigation with fertilization, aeration, transition, pre-emergent windows and pruning by species.

Certified Water Auditor

Water performance is overseen by a Certified Water Auditor through the Irrigation Association.

Watering coordinated with mowing

Because the same organization manages turf and irrigation, watering is coordinated with mowing for cleaner cuts, healthier turf and water savings.
