How we work

How we keep the water honest

Water usage budget

A month-by-month budget in gallons and dollars, built from reference ET and plant coefficients. Every month we log what was actually used against it.

ET run-time calculator

Every station gets its own run time, figured from ET, precipitation rate, distribution uniformity, soil intake and root depth.

Seasonal landscape calendar

A 52-week calendar that sequences irrigation with fertilization, aeration, transition, pre-emergent windows and pruning by species.

Certified Water Auditor

Our program includes oversight by a Certified Water Auditor through the Irrigation Association.

Watering coordinated with mowing

The same crew runs the mowers and the controllers, so we don't water the day before we mow. Cleaner cuts, healthier turf, less water.

Water budgeting and reporting

We can show you the math.

Our program includes a month-by-month water budget for your property, built from reference ET and plant coefficients, with actual use logged against it.

Run times are set station by station, and you can see where every number came from.
