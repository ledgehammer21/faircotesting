Communication and reporting

You hear from us before you have to ask.

Daily communication

A reliable daily touchpoint with your on-site supervisor and account manager, so issues are resolved at the right level.

Executive dashboards

A landscape health dashboard with at-a-glance visibility into water usage, ET variance, repairs completed, quality scores and upcoming priorities.

Transparent reporting

Reports delivered on a set cadence, with completed, ongoing and upcoming work clearly noted.

QR code verification

Tagged locations let your staff scan and independently verify inspection details and service history.

Resident engagement

A resident newsletter, seasonal landscaping tips and education classes that keep residents informed and connected.

Proactive updates

We surface conditions, recommendations and upcoming needs before they become problems.

Technology working in the landscape

Smarter tools. Better information. Better outcomes.

We evaluate and deploy technology where it can improve consistency, efficiency, safety, sustainability and the experience of the communities and properties we serve.

Autonomous mowing

Consistent turf maintenance while skilled team members focus where judgment and horticultural expertise provide greater value.

Battery-powered equipment

Commercial backpack blowers, chainsaws, hedge trimmers and other quieter equipment for sensitive areas.

Drone program

Aerial inspections, storm assessments, progress documentation, canopy observations and planning applications.

Smart landscape technology

GPS mapping, smart controllers, 2-wire systems, flow sensors, master valves and digital reporting.

Technology doesn't replace experience. It makes experienced people more effective.
