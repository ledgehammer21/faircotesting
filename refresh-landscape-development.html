Talk to our team

The same team from planning to closeout

Since 1978, Fairco has worked alongside real estate developers and homebuilders across Arizona, California and Nevada. That history gives our team a working knowledge of the challenges developers face, and practical ways to solve them.

The team that bids the project is the team that builds it, from initial planning through construction and closeout. We self-perform the landscape and irrigation work with our own crews and equipment, and bring in trade partners we've worked with for more than 20 years for everything else.

Services

Everything a master-planned community, multifamily or commercial landscape needs, from the first site walk to final establishment.

  • Pre-Construction & Project Management

    Submittals, approvals, a schedule with key milestones, and accurate estimates that put your budget where it matters and avoid surprises.

  • Landscape Installation

    Trees, shrubs, ground covers, turf and native species, installed by our own crews from soil prep through final establishment.

  • Irrigation Systems

    Main lines, laterals, drip, spray, rotors and smart controller integration, with full pump station installation capability.

  • Lakes, Water Features & Pump Stations

    Community lakes, detention basins, water features and pump stations, built to the engineer of record's plans.

  • Native Plant Salvage

    Salvage programs that meet Maricopa County and City of Scottsdale requirements, from species inventory and GPS mapping through boxing, nursery care and replanting.

  • Rock Work & Material Processing

    On-site rock screening and crushing, plus DG, rip-rap and decorative rock supply and installation.

  • Hardscape, Boulders & Site Furnishings

    Boulder walls and decorative rock work, plus ramadas, shade structures, benches and other site amenities.

  • Landscape Lighting & Low-Voltage

    Landscape lighting, irrigation controls and related low-voltage systems, with line-voltage work through licensed trade partners.
