How we look after a property

You have enough on your plate. Fairco has built landscapes since 1978, and we manage them the way an owner would: as an asset with a value to protect and a plan that runs years ahead.

We treat the landscape as one system: the turf, the trees and the water that feeds them. Regular inspections, a written plan and the same crew week after week mean needs get caught early, and you hear about them before they cost money.

Our services

Five services and one accountable team. Each one is available on its own, and they work best together.

Landscape Maintenance

See what we see.

You shouldn't discover a landscape problem before we do. Fairco documents conditions, communicates priorities and gives property managers and boards the information they need to make decisions. Our program follows Sustainable Landscape Management (SLM) standards, as recommended by the Arizona Landscape Contractors Association.

  • Site inspections with QR code verification
  • Turf, plant & bed care
  • Irrigation management & repairs
  • Fertilization, weed & pest management
  • Seasonal planning
  • Enhancement recommendations
  • App-based reporting
  • Long-term landscape planning

Arbor Resources

Your trees are an asset. Care for them that way.

ISA Certified Arborists direct every tree job, and the work follows ANSI A300 pruning standards. We build a GIS-based inventory of every tree, prune each species in its correct season and assess risk using the ISA Tree Risk Assessment method.

  • Tree inventories & assessments
  • Structural & young-tree pruning
  • Tree risk assessment (TRAQ)
  • Tree removal
  • Cabling & bracing
  • Monsoon preparedness
  • Storm-damage cleanup
  • Multi-year plans & budget forecasting

Plant Health Care (PHC)

The right work, at the right time, for the right reason.

Healthy plants are the result of someone paying attention. Our program leads with Integrated Pest Management: monitoring, cultural and mechanical controls and biological options first, with targeted, correctly labeled treatment only where it's warranted. Seasonal soil testing sets fertilization rates and product selection.

  • Soil & root-zone evaluation
  • Fertilization & soil health
  • Insect & disease identification
  • Insect & disease management
  • Systemic treatments & trunk injections
  • Pre- & post-emergent programs
  • Tree health monitoring

Irrigation & Water Management

Manage the water. Improve the landscape.

Water is one of the biggest lines in a landscape budget, and the one with the most room to move. We audit the system, build a month-by-month water budget from historical evapotranspiration and set run times from real-time ET, replacing what the landscape loses. Water Management is available on its own or as part of a Landscape Management contract.

  • Initial water audit
  • Water budgeting
  • Historical & real-time ET management
  • ADWR schedule compliance
  • Controller programming & management
  • Smart controllers & 2-wire systems
  • Flow sensors & master valves
  • Leak & water-waste identification
  • Conservation & conversion recommendations
  • Certified Water Auditor oversight

Landscape Enhancements

Strategic enhancements. Lasting landscape value.

Because we manage the property as a connected system, our recommendations weigh more than appearance: water use, infrastructure, site structures, horticultural performance, maintenance requirements, lifecycle cost and your long-term goals.

  • Landscape & planting renovations
  • Turf reductions & conversions
  • Tree replacement programs
  • Irrigation modernization
  • Smart controller upgrades
  • Granite & rock refresh
  • Entry & monument improvements
  • Amenities & site structures
  • Water conservation projects
  • Capital planning

You'll know the work got done

Most maintenance headaches start the same way: nobody's sure what got done, or when. Fairco documents the work as it happens and reports on a set schedule. Our goal is to have the detail reports in your hands before board meetings and resident updates.

  • Daily communication

    A reliable daily touchpoint with your on-site supervisor and account manager, so issues are resolved at the right level.

  • Transparent reporting

    Reports on a set schedule, with completed, ongoing and upcoming work clearly noted.

  • QR code verification

    Tagged locations let your staff scan a code and check inspection details and service history.

  • Proactive updates

    You hear about conditions and upcoming needs from us first, before they become problems.
