How we work

How we do it

Dedicated teams

Mowing, irrigation and pruning each have their own crew. Everybody owns something.

The same crews, the same areas

The same people work the same areas. They learn the plants, and they notice when something's off.

Natural-form pruning

We prune to the shape the plant wants to be. No boxes, no balls, and only in the right season.

Prevention first

The right pre-emergent, at the right rate, at the right time. Weeds you never see.

Soil testing sets the program

We test the soil each season and feed it what it's missing, instead of guessing.

QR code verification

Scan the tag at any location and see when we were there and what we did.
